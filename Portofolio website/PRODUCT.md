# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UI/UX and product design teams hiring interns and junior designers are the primary audience (confirmed by Costin, October 2026). They arrive from a CV or LinkedIn link, usually on a laptop, with a minute or two to decide whether to read a case study or move on.

Game studios and front-end teams are secondary audiences. The games show range, systems thinking and prototyping skill to all three.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Costin Sarghiuta, a student who works across UI/UX design, game design and front-end development. It exists to get him internships and junior roles. Success is a visitor who opens a case study or plays a game, then gets in touch.

## Positioning

Costin designs a thing, tests it on real people or with data, then builds it himself. The proof is on the site: games you can play in the browser, and case studies that show what each round of testing changed.

## Operating Context

- Hosted on GitHub Pages at www.costinsarghiuta.com, built with Vite and React into `../docs`.
- Three games are served from `public/` and playable in the browser: Muster (`/muster/`), River Racer (`/game2/`) and Duck Shoot (`/game/`).
- The contact form posts to Web3Forms. The CV is `public/cv.pdf`, served at `/cv.pdf` and made from `cv/cv.html` (the command is at the top of that file).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no backend. Everything must work as a GitHub Pages build.
- Visitors must always be able to reach the work, the CV and the contact details without playing along with any interactive idea.
- Works on phones as well as laptops.

## Brand Commitments

- The name Costin Sarghiuta and the role line "Game designer & UI/UX designer".
- The status line "Open to internships and junior roles".
- No fixed visual identity. Costin asked for a full redesign that is as creative as possible, and for a result that does not look AI-made.

## Evidence on Hand

- Muster: a 5-minute, two-player dice strategy duel made in Unity. Balanced with about 24,000 simulated bot matches (win rates: balanced 67%, elite 61%, counter 58%, all-Barbarian 15%). Screenshots in `src/assets/muster-*.jpg`.
- ShiftPro: a shift-swap app designed in Figma for Primark staff. Two interviews, three prototype rounds, 3 lo-fi testers and 9 hi-fi testers. Screenshots in `src/assets/shiftpro-*.jpg`, clickable prototype linked.
- Calorie App and Finance Dashboard: Figma prototypes.
- River Racer and Duck Shoot: phone-controlled browser games in p5.js with WebRTC.
- Earshot: a macOS menu bar app in Swift that routes each app's sound to its own speaker. Routes switch on in 7 to 14 ms.
- Live Finance Dashboard: Python and Streamlit, deployed.
- Portrait in `src/assets/profile.jpg`.
- No testimonials, client logos or employer names exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the proof. Show it running or show the test result, never describe it in adjectives.
2. A visitor in a hurry gets to a case study in one click.
3. Process is the differentiator: what was tested, what was found, what changed.
4. Playful is welcome. It must never read as childish, and it must never block the way to the work.
