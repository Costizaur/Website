The idea

The site opens inside its own editor, the way a game looks while you are building it. The hierarchy is the menu, the inspector holds each project's facts, and the console prints what testing found.

The idea

The page reads like your working document. Red pen notes point at real results: 24,000 simulated matches, 12 usability tests, and what changed after each round.

How it moves

Notes draw themselves in as you scroll, and a switch in the menu hides them for a clean read. Charts are built from your real numbers.

Trade-off

The strongest proof of process and the calmest look. It is less of a show than the other two.
