Game designer & UI/UX designer, open to internships and junior roles. Every folder below is its own world. Open one.

    The idea

    The four folders stay, and each one is now a world. Open a folder and its colour pours over the screen, its typeface takes over and the projects fly out of it as sheets. Click a sheet and it grows until it is the whole screen.

    How it moves

    The folders shrink into a sidebar. Going back runs the same path in reverse, and you can turn it around halfway. Escape goes back at once with no animation, the way your site does now.

    Kept from your site

    The folder drawing with icons peeking out, the hover fan, the four folder colours, the Finder sidebar and the breadcrumb path. The unfold-the-process paper would sit on the project screen.

    Not in this sketch

    The phone layout, About, Contact, and the pages behind the case study buttons. Play and prototype buttons open your live site or Figma in a new tab.
